The hero ingredient

Why olive leaf

Olive-leaf extract carries oleuropein, a polyphenol studied for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. In skincare, it's one of the gentler ways to calm visible redness without reaching for stronger actives.

  • Cold-extracted from leaves, not fruit, for a higher polyphenol yield
  • Sourced from small groves in the Mediterranean basin
  • Paired with squalane to help the skin hold on to moisture
Is Elaris for you

Built for three kinds of reactive skin

Redness-prone

Skin that flushes easily with heat, weather, or new products, and needs calming before anything else.

Dehydrated

Skin that feels tight by midday despite regular moisturizing, often in humid or air-conditioned climates.

Post-treatment

Skin recovering from facials, peels, or retinoids, that needs a simple routine while it settles.
